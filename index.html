What We Focus On

Our Core Pillars of Impact

We implement target solutions that empower local communities and promote self-sufficiency.

Sustainable Education Infrastructure

Our educational programs focus on building school structures in remote villages and supplying classroom materials directly. We hire local native tutors who understand the culture and needs of the children.

  • Primary school construction & renovation
  • Textbooks, uniforms, and stationary supplies
  • Native language and literacy development camps
Read Our Strategy

Gravity Aqueducts & Clean Springs

Clean water is a fundamental right. In hilly and isolated terrains, traditional wells are impossible. GCF designs gravity-fed water networks that guide water directly from mountain streams to village distribution points.

  • Constructing water filter containment tanks
  • Running pipelines directly to central village taps
  • Training local panels in plumbing maintenance
See Piping Technicals

Reforestation & Eco-Farming

Deforestation destroys watersheds and local food security. We collaborate on native seed plantings and agroforestry methods, combining fruit crops with forest expansion to create sustainable sources of income.

  • Planting native tree seedlings to restore forest covers
  • Establishing agroforestry models for village families
  • Conducting climate resilience training workshops
Learn About Reforestation

Protecting Indigenous Legacies

Indigenous communities hold ancient conservation wisdom. We work alongside local elders to record oral traditions, publish materials in traditional scripts, and ensure their lifestyle remains resilient.

  • Supporting script script classes for youth
  • Sponsoring traditional craftsmanship & loom weaving
  • Documenting ecosystem management methodologies

Cultural Roots

Supporting the Chakma Indigenous People

The Chakma are the largest indigenous community in the Chittagong Hill Tracts. They maintain a beautiful heritage, unique written alphabet script, and sustainable eco-centric lifestyle. Modern changes threaten their scripts and remote regions often face basic health and water access challenges.

GCF collaborates directly with village chief councils to implement script tutors, support maternal clinics, and install pipelines without disrupting their heritage.
