Our Initiatives

Education: The Foundation of Social Transformation

At Global Compassion Foundation, education is not viewed as a privilege, but as a basic human right that every child deserves, regardless of economic status or place of birth.

Our Education Initiatives focus on providing quality, accessible, and compassionate education to underprivileged children living in rural and marginalized communities in Mizoram, with special attention to areas where opportunities are limited.

Explore Our Mission

Charitable School for Underprivileged Children

Establishing a non-profit charitable school at Tuichawng Village, Mizoram — primarily for underprivileged Chakma children, addressing poverty and distance barriers with sincere, caring education.

  • Classes from Nursery to Class X (phased development)
  • Minimal fees ensuring access for all families
  • Safe, supportive, and child-friendly environment

Our Educational Approach

We focus on real learning outcomes and value-based development, helping children from all backgrounds break the cycle of poverty with knowledge, confidence, and hope for a better future.

  • Small class sizes for individual attention
  • Value-based curriculum with ethical guidance
  • Inclusive learning — zero discrimination

Boarding & Support Facilities

To support children from distant villages, the Foundation plans to gradually establish hostel and care facilities ensuring safe lodging and full physical and emotional well-being.

  • Separate safe lodging for boys and girls
  • Nutritional support and basic healthcare
  • Counseling and mentorship for all students

Vision & Transparency

We aim to develop the school into a model rural institution with vocational training and digital learning, while ensuring complete financial accountability and community involvement.

  • Digital integration in learning (phased)
  • 100% of contributions spent on children's welfare
  • Full financial accountability and transparency
Who We Are

Bypassing bureaucracy, driving change

Global Compassion Foundation is built on a 100% direct-impact model. We are a lean team working with local village councils to execute projects directly on the field. Every rupee donated goes straight to materials, tools, and teachers — no administrative overhead.

  • Zero bureaucracy — every donation reaches the community
  • Local leadership — all projects verified by village elders
  • Founded by local educators with firsthand knowledge

Cultural Roots

Supporting the Chakma Indigenous People

The Chakma are a proud ethnic tribal community of Buddhist heritage with a distinct culture, unique written alphabet script, and a sustainable eco-centric lifestyle rooted in Northeast India for centuries.

GCF collaborates directly with village chief councils to implement script tutors, support maternal health, and install pipelines — all without disrupting the heritage and traditions of the community.
