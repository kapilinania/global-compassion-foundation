Direct Model
Who We Are

Grassroots action for social change

We bypass heavy administrative layers, delivering 100% of public donations directly to field operations and building materials in rural hamlets.

100% Direct Impact Model

Every cent donated goes explicitly to buy physical books, clinic kits, and water pipes.

Community Ownership

Local village councils manage, install, and audit GCF-funded project operations.

Eco-Conscious Integration

All constructions utilize local sustainable assets and native planting initiatives.

Cultural Support

The Chakma Community

We collaborate with local elders in Chittagong Hill Tracts and Northeast regions to build resilient programs while protecting their heritage.

Chakma Script Classes

Preserving a unique literary heritage. GCF funds local tutors to teach school-aged youth reading and writing in native Chakma scripts.

Gravity Aqueducts

Bringing safety and hygiene. Installing durable, gravity-fed pipeline networks from mountain springs to isolated hilltop villages.

Mobile Care Clinics

Closing the medicine gap. Funding mobile health camps and essential medicines to treat endemic fevers and malaria in isolated zones.
