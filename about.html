Our Mission & Team

Bypassing bureaucracy to fund water, schooling, and ecosystems directly into isolated indigenous communities.

Pledging Transparency

Our Vision for Direct Action

Global Compassion Foundation (GCF) was founded by a coalition of field coordinators and humanitarian engineers who grew tired of standard non-profit administration. We realized that up to 65% of international donations were lost in overheads, marketing campaigns, and middle-man layers.

Targeted Field Delivery

We do not lease office buildings or run heavy media operations. Funds buy materials deployed directly by village councils.

Equitable Partnerships

Every project is proposed, managed, and monitored by indigenous village chiefs (Elders Council) to ensure cultural safety.

How We Started

GCF Milestone Journey

A look back at our growth and historical checkpoints in the field.

2018

Foundation & Inception

GCF was established by humanitarian field coordinators, launching our first direct-impact education project in isolated Sajek Valley hamlets.

2020

Pledging the 100% Model

We restructured to a strict direct-impact methodology, guaranteeing that 100% of public fundraising purchases raw tools and material resources directly.

2022

Gravity Water Pipelines

GCF engineers launched the gravity pipeline systems, eliminating daily cliff climbs for water in 8 mountainous villages.

2024

Script Preservation Initiative

We began financing classes, textbooks, and tutoring camps to teach children ancestral Chakma written scripts, protecting indigenous heritage.
