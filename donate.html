FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding Section 80G tax exemptions, payment methods, and utilization of funds.

How do I claim my 80G Tax Exemption?

Select the "Claim 80G Tax Exemption" option in Step 2 and enter your PAN. Once the donation is verified, GCF generates your Form 10BE certificate and sends it to your registered email and WhatsApp. This certificate is all you need to claim your 50% tax exemption under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act.

Which UPI apps are supported for QR codes?

You can scan the dynamic QR code using any Indian UPI app, including Google Pay (GPay), PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Mobikwik, and your official banking apps (YONO SBI, iMobile, etc.).

Is it safe to enter my card and bank details?

Absolutely! All card details and Netbanking transactions are encrypted using secure 256-bit SSL connections and processed via top-tier Indian payment gateways (like Razorpay/UPI), ensuring maximum security. GCF never stores your financial information.

How is my donation utilized?

GCF is founded on a 100% direct-impact model. This means 100% of public donations are used to purchase project supplies (like aqueduct pipes, cement, books, seeds, and medical kits) directly from verified local vendors, without any administrative overhead.
