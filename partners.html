Collaborative Impact

Strength Through Partnerships

At Global Compassion Foundation, we believe that sustainable development is built on collaboration. Working with strategic partners allows GCF to mobilize skills, optimize workflows, and maintain a standard of service that changes lives.

Our collaborations range from volunteer recruitment channels to structural audits that help us evaluate our workplace culture. By aligning GCF with industry-leading entities, we bring advanced infrastructure, verified management practices, and global volunteer resources to the remote Chakma tribal schools of Tuichawng in Mizoram.

Volunteering Partner

ConnectFor — Bridging the Volunteering Gap

ConnectFor acts as GCF's primary pipeline for recruiting skilled professionals and passionate mentors across India.

Through ConnectFor's digital matching platform, GCF is able to publish structured volunteering requirements and attract specialized individuals. Whether we need remote curriculum designers, content writers, or on-site teachers to conduct English and science classes in deep forest tribal villages, ConnectFor helps us connect with the right talent to expand our operations.

Key Collaboration Impact Areas:

Academic Mentors

Conducting online English and STEM coaching sessions.

Curriculum Creators

Designing modern syllabi and interactive textbooks.

Field Coordinators

Directly supporting logistics and operations on the ground.

Digital Literacy Advisors

Structuring basic computer skills classes for children.

Workplace Excellence

Certified as a Great Place to Work

GCF has been officially recognized as a certified Great Place to Work in India from May 2026 to May 2027.

This certification represents our commitment to creating an outstanding workplace culture. Working in deep tribal sectors like Tuichawng, Lunglei presents logistical and emotional challenges for our field staff. By offering support, transparent communication, and equal growth opportunities, we ensure that GCF remains a safe, empowering, and motivating environment for every educator, volunteer, and coordinator.

Educational Partner

My Children Foundation — Give Them Wings

GCF collaborates with My Children Foundation to provide supplementary academic support, structured mentorship, and financial scholarships for tribal children in remote regions.

This partnership bridges the learning gap for students in rural villages by establishing local tuition hubs and pairing students with online mentors. By offering financial aid and holistic guidance, we empower students to complete their schooling and pursue higher education, enabling them to "spread their wings" and break the cycle of generational poverty.

Key Alliance Focus Areas:

Supplementary Tuition

Establishing local learning centers with trained community facilitators.

Student Mentorship

Connecting tribal youth with virtual mentors for career and personal guidance.

Financial Scholarships

Sponsoring school fees, textbooks, uniform costs, and learning materials.

Livelihood Coaching

Providing vocational training and livelihood guidance for student families.
