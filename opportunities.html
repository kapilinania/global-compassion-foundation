Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about eligibility, funding coverage, visas, and submitting competitive international applications.

The "Fully Funded" programs listed here cover your primary costs: round-trip economy airfare from your home country, hotel/campus accommodation, daily meals, local airport transfers, and in many cases (such as the Future Diplomats Forum Geneva and New York Conference) visa fee reimbursements or official J-1 visa sponsorship.

Absolutely. Almost all of these programs — including the Stanford Fisher Family Summer Program, European Youth Assembly, USTC Winter Camp, and Universal Youth Leadership Summit — explicitly invite applications from candidates worldwide, with an emphasis on diversity, emerging leadership potential, and underrepresented communities.

While some programs allow you to apply while your passport is being processed, having a valid passport with at least 6 months of validity is strongly recommended. Once accepted, host organizations require your passport details immediately to book flight tickets and issue official visa invitation letters.

Most international youth forums, summits, and fellowships do not strictly require formal IELTS or TOEFL test scores if your university or school medium of instruction was English. An English Proficiency Certificate from your college or institution is typically accepted.
